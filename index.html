What in the Niche Are You Doing?

A niche is a specialized segment of a broader market — focused on a specific audience with distinct needs and preferences. Choosing yours is the single most important step you'll take in this journey.

Your niche = the problem you solve — not the person you solve it for.

Figure out the problem you want to solve for people. Then pick something you could genuinely talk about for hours, because consistency is everything.

The Top 10 Niches

💰 Personal Finance 🌿 Health & Wellness 🍽️ Food & Drink 💪 Fitness ✈️ Travel 🏠 Home Improvement 💕 Relationships 📚 Education 👶 Parenting 🌲 Outdoor Lifestyle
✦ Mama Tip

Pick the niche that lights you up. You'll be creating content around it daily — choose something you actually love talking about!

Chapter Nine

09

TikTok Strategy

The reach on TikTok right now is extraordinary. But you have to be strategic — users are ready to buy low-ticket offers, but they do not want to feel sold to.

  • 1

    Create a Fresh Personal Account

    Don't use your old scroll account. Create a new one — NOT a business account (they get fewer views). Keep it niche-only.

  • 2

    Same Username Everywhere

    Use the same handle across TikTok, Instagram, and Pinterest so followers can find you easily.

  • 3

    Hit 1K Followers First on TikTok

    This unlocks the LIVE feature and lets you add a link in bio — which is where your sales happen.

  • 4

    Post Personal Storytelling Content

    TikTok is rewarding authentic, voice-over storytelling. Short-form (7–10 seconds) still gets the most engagement.

🚫
Don't do follow trains — they won't give you engaged followers. Quality over quantity every time.
✦ Mama Tip

Your first 90 days on TikTok is about testing hooks and finding your voice. Don't chase viral — chase connection.

"It takes an average of 7 touchpoints before someone purchases from you."

Chapter Eleven

11

Pinterest — The Long Game

Pinterest is a search engine, not just social media. Pins can drive traffic to your product for years. Start posting now — your future self will thank you.

  • 1

    Do Keyword Research

    Search your niche topics in Pinterest and note what people are already searching. Keep a running list on your phone.

  • 2

    Convert to a Business Account

    Claim your Stan Store link as your website. Write your bio using the keywords you researched + a clear call to action.

  • 3

    Create 10 Boards

    Use simple, searchable names. Think like your ideal buyer would search.

  • 4

    Post Daily — Start with 1 Idea Pin

    Work up to 10–15 pins per day. One piece of content can become 5–10 pins by swapping the image and title.

📌
Idea Pins are being pushed more than static pins right now — take advantage of this while the algorithm favors them!
✦ Mama Tip

Pinterest is the only platform where a post you made 2 years ago can still be your best-performing piece of content today. Plant seeds early.

Chapter Sixteen

16

Get Your Name Out There

Some people say not to interact with others in your niche — but that's wrong. You want to be the authority figure who is visible, supportive, and knowledgeable.

  • ✦

    Engage with Your Niche Community

    Comment, collaborate, support. Position yourself as someone who is genuinely invested in the community.

  • ✦

    Do Collabs & Lives

    Reach out to people you know. Ask them to do a live with you on a topic related to your offer. Borrowed audiences are gold.

  • ✦

    List Everywhere

    Don't limit yourself to social media. List your product on Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad — everywhere your buyer might be searching.

The more eyes on your product, the more sales you'll make. Visibility is a strategy. Show up everywhere.

✦ Mama Tip

Your biggest competition isn't another creator. It's invisibility. Show up, comment, collaborate — let people find you everywhere they look.
